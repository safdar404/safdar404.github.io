04 — How I build

Useful AI is bounded, cited and reviewable.

Fourteen years of government and infrastructure GIS taught me that decisions need provenance. I carry that into every model, agent and map I ship.

  • Evidence first. Retrieval and citations before generation.
  • Humans approve. Agents propose; named reviewers decide.
  • Assumptions labelled. Modeled overlays and scenarios are never presented as surveyed fact.

Tool calling & bounded agent loops

HealthAssist, CareerRainbow, MarketMind and GharBuilder use project-specific tools with review controls and approvals.
