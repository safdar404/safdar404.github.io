Reproducible GIS workflow

  1. Use a reviewed field boundary in WGS84; reproject for area calculations.
  2. Use atmospherically corrected Sentinel-2 L2A imagery. Mask cloud, cirrus, shadow, snow and invalid pixels using SCL.
  3. NDVI = (B08 − B04) / (B08 + B04).
  4. NDMI = (B08 − B11) / (B08 + B11); align 20 m and 10 m grids explicitly.
  5. Exclude boundary mixed pixels; report the median, cloud and valid coverage for each date.
  6. Compare the same field and crop stage, then verify anomalies on the ground.

Small fields and narrow plots may have too few uncontaminated pixels for dependable monitoring.

Quality rules in this portal

Cloud cover above 20% or valid coverage below 70% flags a measurement for review. These configurable-workflow defaults are screening rules, not universal scientific thresholds.

Charts retain flagged values with a different colour, but comparisons exclude them. No crop health score is inferred from a single NDVI threshold.

Pesticide application record

Review effects, not assumptions

Before/after changes do not prove a treatment caused recovery. Weather, irrigation, crop stage, sampling differences and untreated controls matter. Residue safety requires laboratory testing.

Integrated pest management

Scout first. Identify the pest, compare with a local action threshold, protect beneficial organisms and consider cultural or biological controls. Rotate modes of action only according to current label and extension advice.

Application & harvest interval ledger

Dates calculate elapsed label intervals only. They do not certify legal compliance, residue safety or readiness to harvest. Product-specific label rules take precedence.
