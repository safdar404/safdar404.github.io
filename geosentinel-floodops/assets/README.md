# AgriSentinel conceptual artwork

Three project assets generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 21 October 2026. The original PNGs were preserved; web-ready WebP derivatives retain the composition at 1280 px width. Images are conceptual and are not satellite acquisitions, farm photographs, diagnostic evidence, measured data or representations of a specific farm.

- `crops-v1.webp`: Punjab-style crop landscape. Used in crop/operations page banners and the crop-scouting shortcut.
- `satellite-v1.webp`: conceptual Earth-observation satellite and agricultural landscape. Used in satellite/GIS banners and satellite shortcut.
- `geoai-v1.webp`: conceptual leaf texture and computational network. Used in AI/analytics banners and yield-lab shortcut.

All page banners carry an AI-generated-illustration label. Actual satellite raster output and scientific symptom diagrams remain separate.

## Final prompt set

### Crop image

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a compact premium agricultural GIS web application banner. Create a wide landscape editorial photograph of healthy green wheat and rice agricultural fields in Pakistan's Punjab plains, golden wheat heads in foreground with nuanced real texture, irrigation lines receding toward a calm horizon, refined natural morning light. Composition should remain useful when cropped to a 3:1 panoramic strip, with key crop detail concentrated in the central horizontal band. Sophisticated deep forest green, warm wheat gold, muted teal palette. No people, no text, no logos, no graphs, no fake measurements, no watermarks. This is conceptual illustrative imagery, not evidence from a particular farm.

### Satellite image

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium satellite-monitoring application banner. Wide landscape cinematic but scientifically plausible illustration of a compact Earth-observation satellite above the curve of Earth, solar panels visible, below a detailed mosaic of agricultural fields in the Pakistan / Indus plains region, faint atmospheric rim. Elegant restrained illumination, deep teal space, forest green landscapes, warm gold sunlight. Central horizontal band composition for a 3:1 panoramic crop, visually clean, no text, no borders, no logos, no graphs, no fake numbers, no laser beams. Clearly a conceptual editorial illustration rather than a specific satellite acquisition.

### GeoAI image

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: sophisticated GeoAI analysis banner for an agricultural decision-support dashboard. Wide landscape macro scene of a vibrant green crop leaf and a restrained luminous network of fine teal and warm gold nodes connecting to a translucent abstract field-grid representation in the background. Real leaf texture, scientific editorial aesthetic, subtle computational structure, dark forest-green backdrop with controlled lighting. Avoid science fiction excess; no robot faces, no humanoid brain, no text, no logos, no charts, no numerical values, no fake measurements. Central horizontal band composition that crops beautifully to 3:1. This is conceptual AI-generated illustration, not a diagnosed crop photograph.
